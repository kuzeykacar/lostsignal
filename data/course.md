Course: Mathematical Analysis I (Analisi Matematica I) - Politecnico di Torino, 1st year engineering.
Topics: real numbers and functions; limits of functions and sequences; continuity;
derivatives and their rules; theorems of Rolle, Lagrange and de l'Hopital; study of a function
(monotonicity, max/min, convexity, asymptotes); Taylor polynomials; indefinite integrals
(antiderivatives, constant of integration, integration by parts, by substitution);
definite integrals and the fundamental theorem of calculus; improper integrals;
numerical series; first and second order differential equations.
Today's lecture: indefinite and definite integrals, fundamental theorem of calculus.
